# Phase 6.1 Test Checklist

## Narrative opening
- Enter an existing life
- Confirm World Director 2.0 describes current local circumstances
- Confirm occupation/relationships/events/opportunities appear only when actually present
- Confirm 3–5 suggested actions appear
- Confirm Do something else focuses the freeform box
- Confirm suggestions fill the action box and can be edited

## Speed
- Try Eat, Rest, Work, Meditate and simple local Travel in Connected mode
- Confirm they use `fast-local` and return immediately
- Try a complex conversation/investigation
- Confirm connected AI is used
- Confirm generation status is visible
- Confirm a slow/failed provider falls back rather than hanging indefinitely

## Persistence
- Advance to another simulated day
- Confirm a new daily scene is created
- Save/reload
- Confirm the life and Director state persist
