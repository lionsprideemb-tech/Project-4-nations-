# Project Four Nations — Beta 2 Test Checklist

## First hour
- Create a new life and confirm guided daily scene
- Try suggested and fully freeform actions
- Test routine fast-local actions
- Test connected AI narration

## Living world
- Advance multiple days and inspect NPC autonomy/social network
- Ignore major world events and confirm autonomous resolution
- Pursue repeated interests and inspect questless story formation
- Return to NPCs after promises/favors/conflicts and test memory callbacks

## Long term
- Advance years on a disposable save
- Inspect children, careers, inheritance and family history
- Inspect settlement districts, industries, damage/recovery and cultural drift
- Switch to a later-generation adult character

## Performance
- Inspect Beta 2 Health after long simulation
- Watch average AI latency/context size
- Confirm save histories remain bounded
- Test save/reload after multi-year jumps
- Run Beta Diagnostics and export a backup
